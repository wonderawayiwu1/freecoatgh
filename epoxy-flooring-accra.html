Frequently asked questions

Is epoxy suitable for Ghana’s climate?

Yes, when installed on a properly prepared dry substrate with a system suited to local conditions.

How soon can I walk on an epoxy floor?

Light traffic is usually possible after the manufacturer’s curing window — we confirm timing on site.
