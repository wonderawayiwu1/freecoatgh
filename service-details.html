Interior & Exterior Painting Accra

FreeCoat Decor (FreeCoat Painting & Decor Co. Ltd) delivers professional interior and exterior painting for homes, offices and commercial buildings across Accra and Greater Accra. Led by CEO Freedom Awayiwu, our crews combine careful surface preparation, climate-ready coatings and tidy site discipline so your finish looks sharp and lasts.

Whether you need a full villa refresh, a shopfront upgrade or room-by-room interiors, we plan colours with you, protect floors and fittings, and apply systems suited to Ghana’s heat, sun and humidity.

  • Colour consultation tailored to Ghanaian light and tropical weather
  • Thorough surface preparation, priming and dust control
  • Interior walls, ceilings, doors, gates, façades and boundary walls
  • Brush, roller and spray finishing for a smooth, even result
  • Clear timelines, daily progress updates and a clean handover

Why homeowners choose FreeCoat for painting in Accra

Accra properties face intense sun, dust and seasonal rains. Cheap labour and thin paint often peel within a year. FreeCoat specifies durable systems, corrects damp or chalky walls before coating, and finishes edges and trim to a professional standard — so you are not repainting sooner than you should.

Our painting process

  1. Site visit & quotation — We assess surfaces, moisture risk and colour goals, then issue a clear scope and price.
  2. Preparation — Scraping, sanding, filling, priming and protection of floors, furniture and landscaping.
  3. Application — Agreed coats with quality materials, including spray finishing where it improves consistency.
  4. Inspection & handover — Walk-through, touch-ups and aftercare tips for washing and maintenance.
