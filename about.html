Our Story

FreeCoat Decor was founded in 2012 by Emmanuel Mensah, a skilled painter and decorator with over 15 years of experience in the construction industry. Starting with small residential projects in Accra, we have grown to become one of the most trusted painting and decoration companies in Ghana.

  • Over 350 completed projects across Ghana
  • Specialized in residential, commercial, and government contracts
  • Expert team of trained painters, decorators, and POP ceiling specialists

Our portfolio includes prestigious projects such as government ministry offices, luxury residential estates in East Legon and Cantonments, hotels, and corporate headquarters. What sets FreeCoat Decor apart is our commitment to using premium materials, attention to detail, timely project delivery, and a team of highly trained professionals who treat every project as if it were their own home.
