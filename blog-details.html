How to Choose a Reliable Painter in Accra (2026 Guide)

  • Freedom Awayiwu
  • 15 Jan 2026
  • Painting Tips

Hiring a painter in Accra should not be a gamble. In 2026, homeowners still face the same risks: vague quotations, poor preparation, fading exteriors and crews that disappear mid-job. As CEO of FreeCoat Decor (FreeCoat Painting & Decor Co. Ltd), I have walked hundreds of sites across Greater Accra. This guide explains how to choose a reliable painter — and protect your investment.

A good finish starts long before the first coat. Ask the right questions, insist on written scope, and judge companies by their process, not only by the lowest price.

“The cheapest quote often becomes the most expensive repaint. Reliable painters show their preparation plan, materials and timeline in writing before work begins.” — Freedom Awayiwu, FreeCoat Decor

1. Start with a clear written quotation

A professional Accra painting quote should list rooms or elevations, number of coats, surface repairs, primer, paint brand or grade, scaffolding or access, and a realistic calendar. If a contractor only gives a single cash figure with no breakdown, you cannot compare offers fairly.

Ask what happens if damp patches, chalking walls or peeling paint appear after scraping. Reliable teams budget for discovery work or explain how variations will be priced.

2. Judge surface preparation, not sales talk

Accra walls collect dust, salt air near the coast, and moisture during the rains. Skipping sanding, filling and priming is the fastest way to peel. Ask specifically:

  • Will they scrape loose paint and treat mildew?
  • How will they fill cracks and level rough plaster?
  • Which primer will they use on new or chalky surfaces?
  • How will floors, furniture and landscaping be protected?

If the answer is “we just paint over”, look elsewhere.

3. Match materials to Ghana’s climate

Interior emulsions and exterior weather coats are not interchangeable. For façades and boundary walls, insist on systems rated for strong sun and humidity. For interiors, discuss washability in kitchens and corridors. A trustworthy painter will explain trade-offs instead of pushing mystery bulk paint with no brand identity.

4. Review recent Accra projects and references

Request photos of completed work in neighbourhoods similar to yours — East Legon, Cantonments, Tema, Kasoa or wherever your property sits. Speak to a previous client about punctuality, tidiness and whether the finish still looks good after a rainy season. FreeCoat Decor publishes project examples on our projects page and gallery so clients can see real Ghanaian sites, not stock photos.

5. Confirm team size, supervision and timeline

Ask who will be on site daily and who inspects quality. A reliable company names a supervisor, agrees working hours, and commits to milestones. Rushing a full villa in an unrealistic number of days usually means thin coats and skipped preparation.

6. Red flags to avoid in 2026

  • Demanding full payment before any materials arrive on site
  • No Ghana contact address, phone or WhatsApp business presence
  • Refusal to put colour choices and coat counts in writing
  • No insurance discussion for high scaffolding or commercial sites
  • Pressure to “start tomorrow” without a site assessment
