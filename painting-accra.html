FreeCoat Decor delivers clean, durable interior and exterior painting for homes, offices and commercial buildings across Greater Accra — since 2012.

Looking for reliable house painters in Accra? FreeCoat Painting & Decor Co. Ltd specialises in interior painting, exterior painting and spray finishing that stands up to Ghana’s heat, humidity and dust.

Our crews prepare surfaces properly — sanding, filling, priming — then apply premium paints with neat cut lines and protected floors. Founder Freedom Awayiwu oversees quality so every wall, gate and façade looks finished, not rushed.

We paint villas in East Legon, family homes in Tema and Spintex, commercial façades and churches across Accra and beyond. Diaspora clients also book FreeCoat remotely with clear updates and photo reports.
